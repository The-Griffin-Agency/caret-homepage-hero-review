HOMEPAGE HERO EXPLORATION

Ten approaches.
One first impression.

These options explore the tone of the homepage hero: focused, approachable, confident, abstract, and collaborative. Each uses the full homepage to show how the image works in context.

The sequence moves from individual moments to more stylized settings, then closes with people connecting. Consider the feeling each image creates, its fit with the CARET palette, and how clearly the headline reads.

Start with V2A →
  1. V2AFocused work↗

    A direct, no-nonsense approach. The subject is absorbed in her work, giving the page a clear sense of purpose.

  2. V2BWarm and approachable↗

    An open expression and relaxed posture make this feel personal and conversational. A warmer introduction to the brand.

  3. V2GA personal connection↗

    A candid smile and an off-camera conversation create an easy, welcoming tone. The emphasis is on the person across the table.

  4. V2EConfident and optimistic↗

    An upward gaze and warm surroundings create a sense of confidence and possibility. More aspirational than task-focused.

  5. V2JDirect and composed↗

    Eye contact gives this option a confident, deliberate presence. The striped shirt brings green into a restrained office setting.

  6. V2CEnergy and movement↗

    A more abstract approach, with people moving through a bright, open scene. The yellow creates energy, though it moves beyond the core CARET palette.

  7. V2DSpace and possibility↗

    A light, architectural approach with people set within a mint-toned environment. Airy and stylized, with the setting doing much of the storytelling.

  8. V2FA cinematic working session↗

    A darker, more atmospheric view of people working together. The green tones connect to the brand, while the lighting creates a more dramatic mood.

  9. V2HA shared perspective↗

    An overhead view puts the group and its shared work at the center. Collaborative and composed, with the green seating anchoring the palette.

  10. V2IEngaged and connected↗

    A bright, engaged expression brings the interaction into focus. The mint shirt and green setting make this a strong fit for the CARET palette.
